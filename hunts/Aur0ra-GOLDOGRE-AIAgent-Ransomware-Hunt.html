Executive Summary

Attacker objective. GOLD OGRE operates Aur0ra (also called Aurora) ransomware: it steals data to an S3-compatible bucket, then deploys the ransomware, and has named at least three victims on its leak site. [#1] [#2]

What is new. Sophos Counter Threat Unit recovered Cursor artifacts showing operators giving AI agents VPN credentials, SOCKS access, targets and objectives. The agents then ran network and Active Directory discovery, analysed credential attack paths, password sprayed, Kerberoasted, coerced and relayed NTLM, abused certificates, searched shares, documents and backups, and reached domain administrator in a subset of environments with little human input. Humans kept control of initial access and of the final ransomware deployment. [#1] [#2]

Guardrails did not stop it. The operators framed the intrusions as authorized red team work or "diagnostics testing". Cursor's Composer model was used alongside models from Anthropic, xAI and Moonshot, with Anthropic used almost exclusively at first. [#1] Model-side guardrails are therefore not a control you can rely on. The defence is on the host, the network and the identity plane.

Why the hunt is hard. The campaign is built almost entirely from legitimate or dual-use software: Cursor, Nmap, NetExec, BloodHound, SharpShares, Certipy, Chisel, Impacket and a renamed S3 client. None of these is malicious on its own, and Cursor on a developer workstation is expected. Every query here is detect-only and every one carries false-positive guidance, because a red team or authorized pentest produces the same telemetry. Only seven IP addresses were published, and no file hashes or domains.

The highest-fidelity signal. The operators are Russian speakers who mistyped commands on a Cyrillic keyboard layout: зы for ps and ды for ls appear in the operator logs. If those strings appear in command history on your network, someone from this operation, or someone replaying their logs, has typed there (Q1).

Defender priority: run Q1 and Q20 over 30 days first, then look for Cursor, VPN clients and offensive tooling on servers and jump boxes (Q2, Q5, Q6). Close the paths the agents used: require SMB and LDAP signing, disable Print Spooler on domain controllers, remove weak Kerberos service-account encryption, audit AD CS, and isolate backups. Do not treat Cursor itself as a detection.

Q1 · Operator keyboard-layout typos: зы and ды (ps and ls typed on a Cyrillic layout)
CONF HIGHFP LOWCOST LOW

Looks for: The two Russian-layout slips Sophos saw in operator logs: зы is ps and ды is ls typed with the wrong keyboard layout. Searches the raw text of console-history and process events for either token used as a command word. Confirm console-history capture is enabled in your tenant. FP: Low. Almost no legitimate command line contains these tokens as standalone words. A Russian-speaking administrator who mistypes in a shell would match, and so would a security team replaying operator logs in a lab.
