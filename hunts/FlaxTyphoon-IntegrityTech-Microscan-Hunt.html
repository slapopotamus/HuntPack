Executive Summary

On 2026-10-08 the US Justice Department and FBI announced court-authorized seizures of the domains behind two hacking tools, Microscan (internet-wide vulnerability scanning) and FishHub (spear phishing and follow-on malware delivery). The DOJ release says the tools were operated and used by actors working for Integrity Technology Group, a company based in the People's Republic of China with PRC government contracts, and that the actors are associated with Flax Typhoon. The same day the FBI, CISA, NSA and partner agencies published joint advisory AA26-281A, and CISA added five CVEs to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a federal due date of 2026-10-11.

The advisory describes a repeatable pipeline: scan with Microscan and open-source scanners, exploit internet-facing software (ProFTPD, ONLYOFFICE Docs, Strapi, Apache Struts, ISC BIND, plus three older KEV entries), run EBurst password spraying and XSS credential harvesting against Microsoft Exchange and Microsoft 365, persist with renamed SoftEther VPN clients (conhost.exe, dllhost.exe), and steal mail with Curlc4 and office-cli. Confirmed Microscan targets include a South Carolina power company, Japanese and Polish airports, Taiwanese gas and power companies and a multinational NGO. FishHub victims include about 20 Taiwanese universities.

What this pack can and cannot do. The six domains named by the DOJ (and a seventh reported by the Hacker News and found in the advisory) are now seized, so DNS and network hits are retrospective evidence of past contact, not live C2. Falcon sees the Exchange and Linux server process chains, Windows endpoint execution of the FishHub-style payload, failed-logon fan-out on Windows and domain controllers, and file hashes. Falcon does not see Microsoft 365 / Entra sign-ins, Exchange IIS logs, web-shell file writes in script languages, the spear-phishing email itself, or a crash of a BIND DNS server. Those are listed as gaps in Section 15 and covered with native hunts in Section 7 where a native log exists.

IOC posture. All 33 indicators ship as detect. No indicator is corroborated by two independent saved sources in a form that would justify prevent, the domains are already seized, and several are legitimate software (SoftEther) or public tools. See Section 10.

Defender priority: patch or isolate any internet-facing ProFTPD, ONLYOFFICE Docs, Strapi, Apache Struts or BIND instance now (KEV due date 2026-10-11), review Exchange and Microsoft 365 for password spraying across many accounts, and run Q1 to Q3 and Q11 over 90 to 180 days of history to find past contact.
