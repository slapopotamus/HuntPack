Executive Summary

SafePay is a closed, operator-run double-extortion ransomware group first seen in September to October 2024. Bitdefender, CloudSEK and Picus report it runs no affiliate program and says so on its leak site, though Blackpoint describes it as a private or semi-private operation, so treat the "no RaaS" framing as the majority view rather than settled fact. Its encryptor shares large parts of the leaked LockBit Black codebase, but researchers stress that code lineage does not establish who the operators are.

Tempo is the story. CloudSEK cites 569 leak-site victims across 47 countries as of 2026-09-17, with Germany and the United States dominant, and Halcyon reports a Q2 2026 rebound that more than doubled the prior quarter's claim total. Several vendors report initial access to encryption in 24 hours or less, though the NCC Group and Huntress cases span two to several days.

Hunt value is the Windows endpoint chain. Entry is valid credentials on VPN gateways and RDP (including a FortiGate local-account MFA bypass), help-desk impersonation over Teams, then ShareFinder.ps1, ScreenConnect and the QDoor loader, WinRAR/FileZilla/Rclone staging, Defender tampering, shadow copy deletion, and an encryptor launched with a distinctive -pass= argument, sometimes through regsvr32.exe. Encrypted files get .safepay and each folder gets readme_safepay.txt. NCC Group also saw hypervisors encrypted.

Defender priority: enforce phishing-resistant MFA on every VPN, RDP and local-account path, turn on EDR and Defender tamper protection, and alert on the pre-encryption chain (ShareFinder, archive staging, Defender tamper, service stops). Regsvr32, rundll32, vssadmin, bcdedit, wevtutil, PsExec and AnyDesk are legitimate tools: hunt their arguments and context, never block the binaries.

Q1 · SafePay encryptor launch flags (-pass= plus path/enc/network)
CONF HIGHFP LOWCOST LOW

Looks for: The encryptor is launched with a mandatory -pass= argument plus -path=, -enc=, -network, -netdrive, -uac or -selfdelete. Sources show it run as an EXE (1.exe -pass= -path= -enc=1) and as a DLL through regsvr32.exe. Matches the argument pattern on any process, so a renamed binary still fires.

Known limitations and deployment notes
  • PowerQuery: The CQL SHA256 column is not projected: tgt.process.image.sha256 is unattested on this schema, so the image path is shown instead.
// HUNT: SafePay encryptor launch flags (-pass= with -path/-enc/-network/-uac)
// MITRE: T1486, T1218.010, T1218.011
// CONF: high  FP: low  COST: low
// REQUIRES: ProcessRollup2 with CommandLine
// LOOKBACK: 7d (set with the console time picker)
// FALSE POSITIVES: none known. -pass= alone is common in unrelated tools, so a second SafePay flag is required.
